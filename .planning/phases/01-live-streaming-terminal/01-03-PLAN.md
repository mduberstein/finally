## Phase Goal

**As a** visitor watching the FinAlly watchlist, **I want to** see each price flash green or red the instant it moves, **so that** I can spot which tickers are active without reading every number.

<objective>
Make price movement visible at a glance: a brief green or red tint on the price cell that fades within about half a second, plus a non-color direction cue so the signal survives for colorblind users.

Purpose: MARKET-02. On a ten-row grid updating twice a second, the flash is what turns a wall of numbers into a readable market.
Output: a tested pure flash state machine, a `PriceCell` component, and the CSS keyframes that drive the fade.
</objective>

## Flagged assumptions

1. **Restart-on-rapid-tick.** No source artifact says what happens when a ticker ticks twice inside the 500ms fade. Assumption: restart from full intensity, since the alternative — ignoring the second tick — would hide movement on exactly the most active tickers. Recorded as a backstop truth because there is no explicit requirement to cite.
2. **Direction glyph as the non-color channel.** The UI-SPEC specifies color for the flash and says nothing about a redundant channel. Assumption: a triangle glyph plus the already-signed percent satisfies the accessibility prohibition without adding UI the spec did not ask for.
3. **Reduced-motion handling.** Not mentioned in any source artifact. Assumption: honoring the preference is correct and costs one media query; the non-color channel means no information is lost when the animation is suppressed.
