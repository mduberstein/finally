## Phase Goal

**As a** trader with $10,000 of simulated cash, **I want to** buy and sell shares at the live market price and watch my cash, positions, and total value respond immediately, **so that** the terminal becomes a place I can actually trade rather than only watch.

<objective>
Complete the trade path in both directions and make every refusal legible: sell fills at the live price and reduces or removes the position, and the three domain rejections come back as the exact inline copy the UI-SPEC specifies, right next to the trade bar.

Purpose: PORT-03, PORT-04, PORT-05, UI-03, TEST-01, and D-02/D-03/D-04. A trading terminal that can only buy is half a terminal, and a refusal the user cannot read is indistinguishable from a bug.
Output: the sell branch and oversell guard in `execute_trade`, the full TEST-01 backend suite including the concurrency race, a pure `frontend/lib/trade.ts` validation module, and a trade bar with Sell, disabled states, and inline errors.
</objective>

The spec-less edge probe returned three items for this plan's requirements as unclassified and unresolved. They are recorded here rather than silently decided.

2. **TEST-01 (unclassified).** The requirement names "edge cases" without enumerating them. Assumption: the enumerated set is RESEARCH.md's requirement-to-test map plus the concurrent-trade race, which RESEARCH.md flags as essential to that language. Frontend component tests are explicitly TEST-03 in Phase 3 and are deliberately not added early; the pure `trade.ts` module is unit-tested because it is a lib module, not a component.
3. **Average cost on a sell.** No artifact states whether a partial sale should re-derive average cost. Assumption: it does not — average cost records what was paid for the shares still held, and a sale realises P&L rather than changing the cost basis. Realised P&L accounting is not a Phase 2 requirement.
4. **Concurrency test mechanism.** RESEARCH.md calls for a concurrent-trade test without prescribing threads or async. Assumption: threads, because the contention being proven is SQLite write-lock contention between two real connections, which a single-threaded async interleave would not reproduce.
