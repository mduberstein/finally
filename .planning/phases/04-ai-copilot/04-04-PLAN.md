## Phase Goal

**As a** trader running a simulated portfolio, **I want to** converse with an AI assistant that analyzes my portfolio and acts on it through natural language, **so that** I can ask questions and place trades in plain English instead of reading tables and filling in forms.

<objective>
Close the loop in the browser: what the assistant actually did shows up as an unmistakable bordered card beneath its message, and the rest of the terminal — cash, total value, positions, heatmap, P&L chart, watchlist, main chart selection — catches up immediately without a reload.

Purpose: the user-facing half of CHAT-03, CHAT-04, and CHAT-05. Plan 03 made the actions happen; without this slice they happen invisibly and the user has to reload to see their own portfolio move.
Output: `ChatActionCard`, the `actionCardText` mapping, and the `onActed` refresh wiring through `page.tsx`.
</objective>
