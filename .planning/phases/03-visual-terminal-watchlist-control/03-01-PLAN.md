## Phase Goal

**As a** trader watching a simulated portfolio, **I want to** see my whole portfolio at a glance and curate which tickers the terminal tracks, **so that** the terminal shows me what I care about and how I am doing without reading numbers row by row.

<objective>
Prove the watchlist-curation path end to end on the server: a ticker posted to `/api/watchlist` persists to SQLite, is picked up by the already-running `MarketFeed` on its next poll, and starts appearing in the price cache — and a DELETE takes it back out. This is the phase's backend tracer; Plan 02 puts a UI on top of it.

Purpose: WATCH-02 and WATCH-03's server half. This is the only slice that crosses the whole backend stack Phase 3 adds (a brand-new package, write routes, the `watchlist` table, and the live feed's ticker list). Proving it first costs one commit if the ownership model is wrong instead of three.
Output: the `backend/app/watchlist/` package owning the entire `/api/watchlist` surface — GET relocated out of `main.py`, plus POST and DELETE — with its own pytest package.
</objective>
